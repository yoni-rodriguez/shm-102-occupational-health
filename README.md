## Course Organization

SHM 102 introduces occupational health through a systems-based,
case-study approach.

Rather than treating occupational hazards as isolated categories,
the course examines worker health across multiple scales:

Society & Policy
↓
Climate & Environment
↓
Communities
↓
Workplaces
↓
Jobs & Tasks
↓
Workers
↓
Body Systems
↓
Cells, Molecules & Microorganisms

Chemical, biological, physical, ergonomic, and psychosocial hazards
are examined across these scales through real-world occupational
health cases.

Cases repeatedly use a common framework:

Worker → Task → Hazard → Exposure → Health Effect → Risk → Controls
